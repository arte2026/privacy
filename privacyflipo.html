This Privacy Policy describes how [Guillermo RM] ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

We believe in privacy by design. Our application is designed to operate with minimal data collection.

b. Information Provided by You

  • Contact Information: If you contact us directly for customer support or feedback, we receive the details you choose to provide (such as your name and email address).

2. Third-Party Services

The App may utilize third-party services that collect information used to identify you or provide essential app functionalities. These third parties have their own privacy policies regarding data handling:

  • Google Play Services: Used for core Android functionality, app updates, and authentication. (Google Privacy Policy)

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, and maintain the App.
  • To improve, personalize, and expand game performance and features.
  • To understand and analyze how users interact with our App.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or bugs.

4. Data Retention and Security

We prioritize the security of your data. We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect any collected information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Anonymous analytical data is retained only as long as necessary to fulfill app improvement goals.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App is suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can promptly delete such data.

6. Your Data Rights & Opt-Out Options

Depending on your location (such as under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, request deletion of, or restrict the use of your data. You can also opt out of personalized advertising at any time through your Android device settings:

  • Go to Settings > Google > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalization (or Reset advertising ID).
